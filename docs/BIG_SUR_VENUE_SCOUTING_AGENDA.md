## 1. Garrapata State Park

- **Where:** ~6.7 mi south of Carmel (Rio Rd), ~18 mi north of “downtown” Big Sur. First major state park south of Carmel on CA-1.
- **Parking:** Pullouts along Highway 1; main lot can fill. No day-use fee at time of writing (check [parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=579)).
- **What to look for (ceremony):**
  - Bluff trails with ocean views and space for ~25 people.
  - Soberanes Point / coastal bluffs for a dramatic backdrop; less “beach” and more “cliff/ocean.”
  - Wind exposure — good to note for hair, mic, and comfort.
  - Sun angle at your ceremony time (e.g. 3 PM) — where will the sun be?
- **Vibe:** Wild coast, less developed, very scenic. Good for an intimate, nature-first feel.
- **Permits:** California State Parks special event permit likely required for a formal ceremony; note contact info for later.

---

## 2. Andrew Molera State Park

- **Where:** South of Garrapata, north of Pfeiffer Big Sur. Entrance on the east side of CA-1.
- **Parking:** Main lot; walk to beach/trails (longer walk than Garrapata).
- **What to look for (ceremony):**
  - Beach access (Molera Beach) — possible for a small group if you want sand/ocean.
  - Meadow and trail areas for a non-beach option with Big Sur backdrop.
  - How far the walk is from the lot (guests in dress shoes, older family).
  - Restrooms and shade availability.
- **Vibe:** More “park” than pure bluff; mix of meadow, trees, and coast.
- **Permits:** Same as above — state park special event permit.

---

## 3. Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park

- **Where:** In the “heart” of Big Sur (CA-1), with Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park entrance; nearby Big Sur village for food/supplies.
- **Parking:** Day-use lot; can be busy on weekends.
- **What to look for (ceremony):**
  - Redwood groves — unique “forest” ceremony option vs. ocean-only.
  - Riverside or meadow spots that could fit ~25.
  - Proximity to Big Sur Lodge / village (restrooms, backup if weather shifts).
  - Feel: more wooded and sheltered; less “open ocean” than Garrapata or Julia Pfeiffer Burns.
- **Vibe:** Classic Big Sur redwoods + river; good if you want a mix of forest and coast in one day.
- **Permits:** State park special event permit; check if they have designated ceremony areas or preferred spots.

---

## 4. Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park

- **Where:** ~37 mi south of Carmel, ~12 mi south of Pfeiffer Big Sur. Southernmost of the four.
- **Parking:** Main lot; short walk to the famous **McWay Falls** overlook (80-ft waterfall onto the beach).
- **What to look for (ceremony):**
  - McWay Falls overlook — iconic photo spot; check if any area there could work for a tiny ceremony (often crowded; permit/restrictions may apply).
  - Ewoldsen Trail and other viewpoints — possible spots with ocean/coast views and a bit more space.
  - Crowd level at the overlook; whether you’d rather use this as a “photo only” stop and hold the ceremony elsewhere.
- **Vibe:** Postcard Big Sur; dramatic and memorable. Best for “wow” photos; ceremony feasibility depends on park rules and crowd management.
- **Permits:** State park special event permit; confirm if ceremonies are allowed at overlook or only in other areas.
- **Note:** Check [parks.ca.gov](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=578) for any closures (e.g. Highway 1 south of this park has had long-term closure in the past).

## Practical tips for today

- **Shoes:** Comfortable walking shoes; you’ll be on trails and possibly sand.
- **Layers:** Coast can be cool and windy; bring a light jacket.
- **Water & snacks:** Few services between Carmel and Big Sur village; pack water and light snacks.
- **Phone / maps:** Service can be spotty; download offline maps or have a rough route written down.
- **Notes:** Take a few photos and short notes at each spot: “ceremony angle,” “wind,” “walk distance,” “restrooms,” “parking for 25.”
- **Permits:** At each park, note the full name and how to look up “Special Event Permit” or “Wedding Permit” for that park so you can apply once you’ve chosen.
